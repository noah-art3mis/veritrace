# Bootstrap eval golds from academic benchmarks, don't scrape the fact-checkers

**Status:** accepted

We need decided, human-adjudicated claims to use as golden examples when scoring the pipeline. The obvious source is the fact-checkers themselves — Snopes, Full Fact, Aos Fatos publish thousands of rated claims. We deliberately do **not** scrape them. Instead we bootstrap golds from openly-licensed academic corpora that already contain these orgs' fact-checks — primarily [AVeriTeC](https://fever.ai/dataset/averitec.html) (English; Snopes + Full Fact among ~50 orgs) and [X-Fact](https://github.com/utahnlp/x-fact) (Portuguese; the route to Aos Fatos) — normalised into one schema. The importers and the full survey live in [`evals/golden/`](../../evals/golden/README.md).

**Why this is surprising:** a future reader sees an eval set sourced from 2021–2023 research datasets when the live sites have fresher, larger, first-party data one HTTP request away, and reasonably asks "why launder the claims through academia instead of just crawling snopes.com?"

**The trade-off we made:** scraping buys volume and freshness, but costs us on every axis that matters for a _trustworthy_ eval. (1) **ToS/legal:** none of the three offer a scraping license; Snopes directs researchers to ask for access, and crawling their article HTML is against the spirit of their terms. (2) **Label quality:** each org uses its own multi-point scale (Snopes True…Mixture…Miscaptioned; Aos Fatos Verdadeiro/Distorcido/Insustentável; Full Fact free text), so we'd be hand-rolling a lossy mapping onto our four verdicts with no inter-annotator agreement behind it — whereas AVeriTeC already normalised to our _exact_ 4 labels (κ=0.62) and X-Fact to a documented 7-way scheme. (3) **Temporal leakage:** a naive scrape pairs a claim with the fact-check article that post-dates it, which silently leaks the answer into retrieval; AVeriTeC was explicitly built to avoid this. (4) **Brittleness:** three bespoke HTML parsers that rot on every site redesign, versus two stable, versioned, citable files. (5) **Structure:** AVeriTeC ships claim→question→evidence→justification, which maps onto our graph for free; raw articles don't. The cost we accept is staleness (no post-2023 claims) and being bound by CC-BY-NC-4.0 on the AVeriTeC slice — acceptable for an internal eval set we don't redistribute.

**Escape hatch (the explicit no):** when we genuinely need fresh, post-2023 claims from these specific orgs, the answer is still not a scraper — it's the free [Google Fact Check Tools API](https://developers.google.com/fact-check/tools/api) (`claims.search` with `reviewPublisherSiteFilter`), which serves the orgs' own ClaimReview markup within ToS and lands in the same `GoldenClaim` schema. A direct HTML crawl of a fact-checker stays off the table.
